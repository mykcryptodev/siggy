How It Works

Set up in under 2 minutes. Seriously.

1

Add the Bot

Add @siggytheparrotbot to your Telegram group. Siggy joins silently and waits for your command!

2

Add Your Safes

Type /addsafe and your Safe address. Siggy starts watching — dutifully!

3

Get Squawked At!

Siggy sends instant alerts when transactions are proposed, executed, or cancelled. Plain English, always!

Bot Commands

Everything Siggy knows how to do

/addsafe <address> — Start monitoring a Safe
/removesafe <address> — Stop monitoring a Safe
/listsafes — See all monitored Safes
/status — Check bot status
/help — Show all commands
